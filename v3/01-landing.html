How it works

An agent, not a search box.

01

Describe the trip

Paste an email, dictate a voice memo, or message us. The way you'd brief a friend who already knows your taste.

02

Approve the plan

We parse your itinerary, fan out across providers, and surface trade-offs where reality doesn't match the brief. You approve once.

03

We execute

Every leg booked. Loyalty applied. Card charged transparently. We monitor for changes and handle them while you sleep.

The difference

Most AI travel tools end with a list of links.
Tures ends with confirmation numbers.

Spec-match over price-shop

You named Real Christine. We book Real Christine. We don't substitute the comparable Marais boutique because it's $40 cheaper.

Transparent, never autonomous on uncertainty

When Finnair doesn't run a non-stop, we surface the two cleanest alternatives with their trade-offs. We never autonomously rebook into something you didn't approve.

Verified twice before "Booked."

Every booking is confirmed against both the provider receipt and the email confirmation in your inbox. We don't say "Booked" until both agree.

In-trip concierge that actually responds

Text Tures from Paris: push my Friday dinner to 9pm. Done — and confirmed — before you've finished your apéritif.
