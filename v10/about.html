Who is Andy Rockwell?›

Not a marketing segment — a specific person:

  • Mid-to-late 30s, $300K+ HHI — finance, tech, law. 12–25 personal travel nights/yr.
  • Knows hotels by name. Asked for Real Christine, not "a boutique in Paris."
  • Status traveler. Airline status, Amex Platinum/Centurion, a Virtuoso advisor he tolerates.
  • Friction-allergic. Eleven minutes to describe the trip, then never think about logistics.
  • High-trust if earned — and gone in 30 seconds if you fake a confirmation.

He's not a price shopper. He's a time-and-cognition shopper.

Jobs to be done›
  • Book exactly what I told you. If I name the hotel, you book that hotel.
  • Find the cleanest feasible alternative when my ask is impossible — and ask first.
  • Handle the chain of permissions. Use my card, credit my status — and tell me what you did.
  • Coordinate dinners — including the ones without a public API.
  • Take care of things while I sleep. Schedule change, strike three days out — handle it.

Business model & honest skepticism›

$1,800/year for unlimited trips + transparent supplier commissions disclosed in the receipt. Slots between Amex Platinum and a Virtuoso advisor.

  • Top-end restaurant inventory isn't fully solvable by software. Honest about autobook vs. request-and-confirm.
  • The Hiccup Handler must never cry wolf. It surfaces a held fix, executes on a real trigger, explains itself every time.
  • Trust collapses on a single visible failure. Never confirm what we can't verify in two places.
  • The LLM never books anything itself. It produces a plan; a deterministic executor validates and submits.
