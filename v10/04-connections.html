Payment

How your card is used

A per-trip authorization with a ceiling you set. Every charge is itemized and pushed to you the moment it's made. The card is held in a PCI-scope tokenization vault — Tures' agent never sees the raw number. Nothing is charged without an authorization you've approved.

Loyalty & status

Applied automatically

AAdvantage, Flying Blue, Finnair Plus and your Virtuoso membership are attached to every booking that supports them — credits applied, status chased mid-trip, upgrades requested at check-in. You see exactly which program was used on each leg.

Calendar

Routes around your life

We read availability to avoid booking over what's already on your plate, then write the confirmed trip back in — flights, hotels, dinners — with confirmation numbers attached to each event. Scoped to free/busy and the events we create.

Inbox

The second source of truth

Read-only, scoped to travel senders only — never marketing, never your personal mail. We use the confirmation email as an independent check against the provider receipt. We don't mark anything "Booked" until both agree.

Messaging

Where the concierge lives

Optional, but it's where most of the relationship happens once you're moving. Connect WhatsApp or Telegram and you can brief Tures the way you'd text a fixer. Same agent, same memory, same permissions as the app.
