What we touch, and what we never keep.

The short version: Tures is an executor. To book your trips and rescue them when the world misbehaves, we read a narrow slice of your digital life — travel email, calendar windows, payment tokens, loyalty balances. We keep the least we can for the shortest time we can, we name everyone who ever touches it, and you can pull the plug in one tap. The long version follows.

IData we collect

We collect only what a trip requires:

  • Trip briefs — the text, forwarded emails, voice memos, or summaries you send describing a trip.
  • Calendar contents — events we read to find and hold the right dates.
  • Email contents — only messages from the travel senders and labels you authorize (see §2).
  • Payment instruments — tokenized through Stripe. Your raw card number never touches our servers.
  • Location data — only while you are on a trip, and only if you opt in to live disruption monitoring.
  • Loyalty accounts & balances — to credit status and apply upgrades you're entitled to.
  • Account & usage data — your name, email, phone, and the actions you take in Tures.

IIInbox & calendar access

Read this part

If you connect your email account, Tures will read messages relevant to travel — confirmations, itineraries, vendor communications, and similar — to coordinate your trip and recover from disruptions. We will not read messages from senders outside the travel domain unless you explicitly direct us to. We do not train any AI model on the contents of your inbox. We do not sell, share, or disclose inbox content to any third party, except as required by law or by the LLM provider processing a specific request on your behalf — in which case only the relevant excerpt is sent. You may revoke inbox access at any time in your account settings, and we will purge cached inbox content within 30 days of revocation or trip completion, whichever is later.

This disclosure also appears at the moment you grant access — not just buried here. We surface a per-grant scope (read-only vs. send-as) and show you, on your account page, when each connection was last used: "we read your Gmail 6 hours ago." Calendar access is treated the same way: we read events to schedule and may write holds for dates we're protecting.

IIIHow long we keep things

  • Trip data — retained for 7 years, consistent with travel-receipt and tax conventions.
  • Inbox & calendar content — retained only for the duration of the trip plus 30 days, then purged.
  • Payment tokens — retained per Stripe's standards; we hold the token, never the card.
  • Audit log — records of agent actions are retained indefinitely for legal defense, and replicated to cold storage. Where you exercise a deletion right, your personal data in these records is redacted (see §5).

IVThird-party processors

We use a small set of named processors to operate Tures. Customer trip content will flow to the LLM providers below — that is how the agent reasons about your trip:

  • Stripe — payments and tokenization
  • Anthropic / OpenAI — LLM inference (sub-processors; trip content is sent per request)
  • Duffel — flight bookings · Expedia — hotels · Viator — activities
  • Browserbase — supervised browser sessions for no-API vendors
  • Resend — transactional email · Twilio — SMS & WhatsApp
  • AWS / Cloudflare — hosting and delivery

This list will grow as Tures adds capabilities. We will update it here and notify you of material additions before your data flows to a new processor.

VYour rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Wherever you live, you may access, delete, port, restrict, and object to our processing of your personal data. Reach us through your account or the contact below and we'll respond within the windows the law requires.

One specific carve-out, stated plainly: records in our agent audit log may be retained beyond a deletion request, under our legitimate interest in legal defense — but with your personally identifying information redacted. We do not sell your personal information.

VIBreach notification

If we discover a personal-data breach that is likely to affect you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities within 72 hours of becoming aware of it — the GDPR floor, adopted as our self-imposed standard everywhere.

VIIContact

Questions, requests, or concerns: privacy@tures.com. For data-subject requests you can also use the controls in your account settings.

This document is a plain-language preview of a policy that a travel-, AI-, and consumer-privacy specialist attorney reviews before deployment. It is written to be honest about a genuinely sensitive product surface; it is not, by itself, legal advice.
