Kill switch

Pause all agent access?

This immediately: invalidates active sessions, halts every running agent, and marks all connected services as revoked. Confirmed bookings remain yours with the vendor. You can re-enable anytime within 7 days before tokens are purged.

Delete account

Delete & purge your data?

We purge inbox & calendar content within 30 days and personal data per your GDPR/CCPA rights. Audit-log records of agent actions are retained for legal defense with your PII redacted. This cannot be undone.
