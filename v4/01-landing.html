The difference

Most AI travel tools end with a list of links.
Tures ends with confirmation numbers.

Spec-match over price-shop

You named Real Christine. We book Real Christine — not the comparable Marais boutique because it's $40 cheaper.

Transparent, never autonomous on uncertainty

When the non-stop doesn't exist, we surface the two cleanest alternatives and their trade-offs. We never rebook into something you didn't approve.

Verified twice before "Booked."

Every booking is checked against both the provider receipt and the email confirmation in your inbox. We don't say "Booked" until both agree.

It knows your taste

The Taste Engine turns a few choices into a working model of how you travel — and sharpens it after every trip.
