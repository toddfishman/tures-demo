Payment

How your card is used

Card numbers are entered into a VGS-hosted field and tokenized before they ever reach Tures — we hold a token, never the PAN. Each booking is a per-trip authorization with a ceiling you set; every charge is itemized and pushed to you the moment it's made. Nothing is charged without an authorization you've approved.

Calendar

Routes around your life

We read free/busy to avoid booking over what's already on your plate, then write the confirmed trip back in — flights, hotels, dinners — with confirmation numbers attached. Scoped to free/busy and the events we create.

Inbox

The second source of truth

Read-only, scoped to travel senders only — never marketing, never your personal mail. We use the confirmation email as an independent check against the provider receipt. Nothing is marked "Booked" until both agree.

Messaging

Where the concierge lives

Optional, but it's where most of the relationship happens once you're moving. Connect WhatsApp or Telegram and brief Tures the way you'd text a fixer — same agent, same memory, same permissions.
