What we watch

A whole board of feeds, scored against your trip.

Wire services

Reuters, AFP and breaking news — the first whisper of anything that moves a route.

Aviation ops & NOTAMs

Airline schedule feeds, gate and equipment changes, and notices to airmen as they post.

Weather models

The major forecast runs, watched for the storm or the clear day before it reaches your window.

Social signals

Early chatter, scored — a rumor surfaces here hours before it reaches an official feed.

Transit & labor

Strike trackers, rail ops and ground transfers — the disruptions that never make the news.

Venue channels

Restaurants, lodges and transfer operators — the closure or the held table, straight from the source.

Every signal is scored against your specific itinerary. The vast majority is filtered as noise before it ever reaches you — you hear from Tures only when there is something worth handling.
