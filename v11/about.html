About

Travel, handled.

We built Tures because planning a great trip still means twenty open tabs and a callback you're still waiting on. Tures is an executor, not a research tool. It doesn't hand you links to go finish — it ends with confirmation numbers.

What we believe

Four principles. No exceptions.

An executor, not a search box

A search engine hands you results. Tures does the work — flights, hotel, the table you actually want — and finishes the job. You describe it once; it returns a trip, booked.

The Brief is the boundary

Your Brief is the authorization line. Tures acts inside it and pauses outside it — when something's ambiguous or pricier than you said, it asks before it acts. Never surprises you with a charge.

No fake success states

Every booking is two-source verified before Tures tells you it's done. If it can't confirm twice, it won't claim it. The confirmation number you see is one we checked — not one we hoped for.

Discreet by default

Your trips, your taste, your documents — held quietly and never traded. Tokenized through VGS, raw values never stored in plain text, and every action audit-logged so you can see exactly what was done on your behalf.

Under the hood

A model that understands you. An engine that books.

Tures splits the work the way it should be split. A language model reads your Brief the way a great concierge would — picking up the cabin you like, the room you mean, the table you'd actually want.

Then it hands a precise plan to a deterministic engine that does the booking. The part that touches your card and your loyalty is exact and repeatable — not improvised by a chatbot.

And every action is logged. You can see what Tures did, when, and why — a complete, calm audit trail of your trip being handled.
