What Tures asks

Six things, then it goes to work.

Origin
Your departure city. Tures never guesses where you start.

Exact days, or a window it can shape around price.
Travelers
Who is going. Two for the anniversary, or the whole family.
Style
Oceanfront and design-forward, or quiet and close to the trail.
Budget
A posture or a cap — business class if it is reasonable.
