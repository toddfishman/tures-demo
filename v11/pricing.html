Good questions.

When am I charged
On Per trip, only when a trip actually books — confirmation numbers in hand. On Concierge, it is $129 a month — or $97 a month billed yearly, saving 25% — with no per-booking fee. Either way, nothing is charged for a trip that does not complete.
What is the Hiccup Handler
The always-on part of Concierge. It watches your flights and the weather, and when something slips — a delay, a cancellation, a storm over your snorkeling morning — it rebooks and moves things around, usually before you would have seen the alert yourself.
Is my card safe
Yes. Payments run through Stripe over 256-bit encryption, and everything Tures stores lives tokenized in the Vault — Tures works from tokens, never your raw card number. You can revoke any of it anytime.
Can Tures really book
It books the whole trip end to end — flights, hotels, cars, tables — and hands you confirmation numbers, not a list of links to go finish yourself. Every booking is verified twice before it is called done. See it run when you plan a trip.
